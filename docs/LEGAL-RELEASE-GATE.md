# Legal and public release gate

The repository remains private and untagged as an official Community release
until every mandatory item has recorded evidence:

- signed gratuitous assignment of identified patrimonial rights to HubTech;
- signed technical annex and authorship/provenance declaration;
- unanimous corporate consent for the related-party transaction;
- confirmed Git author aliases and file-by-file provenance manifest;
- third-party dependency and notice review;
- HubTech and HubICG trademark clearance or accepted risk treatment;
- final review by qualified Brazilian counsel;
- approved baseline commit and explicit publication authorization.

The owner has authorized publication of materials that pass this gate. That
authorization does not make an unchecked item true and does not authorize
signing legal instruments on anyone's behalf.
