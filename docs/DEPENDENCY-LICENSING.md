# Dependency licensing and relicensing procedure

Dependencies are not relicensed as HubICG. For every runtime, build, test,
documentation, model, dataset, font, image and GitHub Action dependency:

1. record name, version/commit, source, author, license and distribution mode;
2. preserve license, copyright and attribution notices;
3. classify compatibility separately for AGPL and commercial distribution;
4. replace, isolate or obtain written permission for incompatible material;
5. record modifications and source-offer obligations;
6. regenerate SBOM, notices and checksums for every release.

Permissive code may usually remain under its original license inside a larger
AGPL work when notices are preserved. Strong copyleft, non-commercial,
source-available, custom, unknown or no-license material requires specific
legal analysis. Do not infer commercial rights from repository availability.
AI-generated material also requires provenance and human review.

Final compatibility conclusions are legal decisions, not scan output.
