# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxing and publication of private data are unacceptable.

Confidential conduct reports may be submitted through GitHub private security
advisories. Prefix the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`; do not include secrets or
unnecessary personal data. The core maintainer will review reports
confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest where possible and document
proportionate corrective action.
